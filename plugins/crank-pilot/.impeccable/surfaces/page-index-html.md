# crank-pilot page

Mode: Operate. The session page for crank and crank-lite: question rounds, readbacks, prototype reviews, and a live agenda, in half a screen beside a terminal.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The session's state is set as type. One enormous figure says how much is waiting on you, and everything else is small, quiet, and exact. It refuses the review-tool default of a grey document with a comment sidebar and a card per item.

OWN-WORLD: Specimen. Paper #f7f6f2, ink #0d0d0d, one accent #ff4f00 spent at most four times per view. A display grotesk at extreme scale against 11-12px uppercase monospace labels. Hairline rules only: no cards, boxes, shadows, or greys. Strikes are ink line-through.

STORY: The user sees at once what is waiting and how much they have marked, reads the artifact on a strict measure, marks it, and sends one structured response.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At 960 px, a left column of about 250 px holds the mark-count numeral at about 220 px, the version slider with its mono readout, and the agenda as SETTLED and OPEN lists. The main column holds a mono breadcrumb, the artifact title at about 44 px, the lead and judgment calls, and the artifact at 16 px. Approve (solid accent) and Send changes sit in a bottom row under one hairline. Signature interaction: the version slider scrubs the artifact between revisions.

FORM: variable-font type specimen; a dealt challenger chosen from the bolder re-roll (round 1), not on the ordered grounded list; seed:70a0cee555d2f417.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
