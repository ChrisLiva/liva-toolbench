# Grilling protocol

The shared interview discipline for walking open decisions to ground with the user. Each skill's flow says *what* to grill and when; this file is *how*.

- **One question per message — exactly one question mark, in plain chat prose.** Not the structured-question UI: plain text leaves room to show your reasoning, and the conversation can follow up on the answer. A sub-question, clarifier, or "and also…" rider is the *next* message, sent after this answer lands.
- **Lead with your recommendation.** Open with your recommended answer and the reasoning or trade-off it accepts, then wait for the response before the next question. Offer discrete options when the choice is genuinely between them — never as a neutral menu; your pick still leads.
- **Explore before you ask.** If the codebase, current docs, or a quick search can settle a question, dispatch a subagent rather than spend the user's attention on it. Reserve questions for what only the user knows — intent, priorities, preferences, external constraints.
- **Settled means settled.** Keep at a question until it's genuinely resolved, not waved past — a hedge or a "we'll see" is not a resolution. And once resolved, don't reopen it on a later question.
